## Registry semantics

- `STANDARD` = formal standard or normative standard-like publication.
- `SPECIFICATION_OR_FRAMEWORK` = specification, framework, modeling language, architecture framework, or community technical specification. These are not mislabeled as ISO/IEC standards.
- Existing Iteration 09 profiles are retained. Their internal provenance is preserved; official authority URLs are added where identified.
- New web-researched records carry an authoritative source URL and `accessed_at: 2026-10-24`.
- No normative standard text is copied into this repository.
- A registry record does not constitute legal applicability, certification, conformity, licensing, or regulatory approval.
