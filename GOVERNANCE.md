# Governance

Canonical contracts are changed deliberately.

Changes affecting entity IDs, schemas, component ABI, safety, compliance,
simulation determinism or lifecycle semantics require review and tests.

Operational authority remains human. AI-generated changes are proposals until
validated and approved.
