Yes. For our platform, dual-use and embargo controls need to be substantially more explicit than a generic export-control disclaimer. They should cover hardware, software, algorithms, technical data, simulation, remote access, cloud services, research collaboration, education, publications, manufacturing, procurement, maintenance, and teleoperation.

The key principle should be:

> Technical capability does not itself establish legal authorization to transfer, provide access to, manufacture, operate, export, re-export, import, publish, or remotely enable that capability.



Dual-Use, Export-Control and Embargo Disclaimer

This platform supports education, research, engineering, simulation, manufacturing, robotics, physical AI, software, hardware, aerospace, marine, industrial, scientific and other technologies that may have both civilian and non-civilian applications.

Some technologies may therefore constitute dual-use goods, software, technology, technical assistance, services, or information under applicable laws or regulations.

> Dual-use classification is a compliance question, not a conclusion of illegality.
