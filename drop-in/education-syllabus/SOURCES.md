Source verification note: source URLs are registry targets. Before ingesting a specific syllabus, verify the live source edition, academic year, language, effective date, licensing/access conditions and exact page/section locator. The source registry does not imply that all contents have been downloaded or reviewed.

Administrative geography and identifiers:
- ISO 3166 official overview and subdivision-code authority: https://www.iso.org/iso-3166-country-codes.html
- UK Government / Permanent Committee on Geographical Names, *India Toponymic Factfile* (2026), including 28 States, 8 Union Territories and their ISO 3166-2 identifiers: https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6a33d3cf25c5199bda11778a/India_Toponymic_Factfile.pdf
- Government of India GST e-Way Bill state-code directory (operational state/UT code cross-check): https://docs.ewaybillgst.gov.in/apidocs/state-code.html

These geographic sources establish jurisdiction identifiers and administrative names only. They do not establish which education boards, SCERTs, examination authorities or curriculum editions operate in each jurisdiction.

Administrative-subdivision discovery data:
- pycountry 24.6.1 ISO 3166-2 subdivision dataset, bundled as a discovery index; validate against the current ISO publisher and national law before operational use. The dataset does not imply each administrative subdivision has a separate education authority or board.
